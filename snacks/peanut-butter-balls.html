Peanut Butter Balls

Four ingredients, no oven, and dangerously easy to make. Crunchy, sweet, and packed with peanut butter.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 15 min
  • Chill 30 min
  • Total 45 min
  • Servings 24
  • Method No bake

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup honey (255 g)
    • Maple syrup — 1:1 Slightly thinner; similar calorie
  • 1½ cups powdered milk (144 g)
  • 3 cups peanut butter (768 g)
    • Almond butter — 1:1 Milder flavor; slightly lower calorie
  • 3 cups Rice Krispies cereal (90 g)

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the peanut butter, Rice Krispies, powdered milk, and honey. Stir until evenly mixed.
  2. Roll the mixture into about 24 balls, roughly 1½ inches each.
  3. Place on a lined baking sheet or plate. Refrigerate until firm, at least 30 minutes. Store in the fridge.

Tips

  • These freeze well. Store in a freezer bag for up to 3 months and thaw in the fridge for 15–20 minutes before eating.
  • If the mixture feels too sticky to roll, chill it for 10 minutes first or lightly wet your hands.
