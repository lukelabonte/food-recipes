# Copy & Pastry

A family recipe collection — tried-and-true dishes from our kitchen to yours, with gram weights, nutrition info, and all the detail you need to cook with confidence.

**Browse recipes:** [copyandpastry.com](https://copyandpastry.com)

## Features

- **Fuzzy search** — Find recipes by name, description, or ingredients. Typo-tolerant, so "chiken" still finds Chicken Noodle Soup
- **Time filter** — Slide to filter recipes by total cooking time when you're short on time
- **Shopping list** — Select multiple recipes, get a merged ingredient list organized by store section. Check items off as you shop — your progress saves between visits
- **Servings scaling** — Adjust the serving count and watch every ingredient, gram weight, and weight estimate update in place
- **Step tracking** — Tap a step to highlight where you are. Previous steps dim so you never lose your place
- **Substitution suggestions** — Tap ingredients to see alternatives with ratios and notes on flavor, texture, and calorie differences
- **Nutrition info** — Every recipe includes nutrition data, labeled as "from source" or "estimated"
- **Print-friendly** — Clean print layout for keeping a paper copy in the kitchen

## How Recipes Are Made

Every recipe is carefully formatted with gram-weight equivalents for all measurements, numbered steps optimized for reading while cooking, estimated cooked and uncooked weights, and tips where they help. Recipes work great with any macro tracking app, or just for anyone who likes precise, well-organized recipes.

## How to Contribute

Have a recipe to share? Visit the [upload page](https://copyandpastry.com/upload.html) to submit it. You'll need a passphrase to upload — request access on that page if you don't have one.

Send your recipe in any format: a photo, a handwritten card, a URL, a video, plain text — whatever you've got.
