Mom's Chili

A hearty, slow-cooked family chili packed with kidney beans, savory ground beef, and zesty pepperoni that deepens in flavor the longer it simmers.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 5 hr
  • Total 5 hr 10 min
  • Servings 6
  • Method Slow Cooker / Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 1 very large can (28 oz) kidney beans, drained (~790 g packaged; drained weight unverified)
    • Pinto beans — 1:1 Slightly creamier texture with a milder flavor; lower calorie density than kidney beans.
    • Black beans — 1:1 Earthier flavor and softer texture; similar calorie profile.
  • 1 very large can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes (~794 g)
    • Diced tomatoes — 1:1 Chunkier texture; same calorie profile but less smooth consistency in the final chili.
  • 1 very large can (28 oz) tomato sauce (~794 g)
    • Crushed tomatoes blended smooth — 1:1 Nearly identical result; same calorie profile.
  • 1/2 pound ground beef (~225 g)
    • Ground turkey — 1:1 Lower calorie and lower fat option; slightly leaner flavor that blends well with the seasoning.
  • 1/2 package (about 2.5 oz) presliced pepperoni (~70 g)
  • 1 package (1.25 oz) Old El Paso or McCormick chili seasoning mix (~35 g)
    • Homemade chili spice blend — 1:1 Combine cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, and cayenne; lower sodium option with the same bold flavor.
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder (~5 g)
    • Ancho chili powder — 1:1 Deeper, slightly smokier flavor with mild heat; same calorie profile.

Steps

  1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, crumble and cook the ground beef until no pink remains, breaking it apart as it cooks. Drain off any excess fat and set aside.
  2. Add the drained kidney beans, crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, cooked ground beef, sliced pepperoni, chili seasoning packet, and chili powder to a large kettle or slow cooker. Stir everything together until well combined.
  3. If using a slow cooker, set it to LOW and cook for at least 5 hours, stirring occasionally if possible. If using a stovetop kettle, bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat, then reduce heat and cook uncovered or partially covered for at least 1.5–2 hours, stirring frequently. The longer it cooks, the better the flavors meld.
  4. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving. Ladle into bowls and serve with tortilla chips, shredded cheese, or sour cream as desired.

Tips

  • The Longer, The Better This chili is best when cooked low and slow. If you have time, let it go 7–8 hours on LOW in the slow cooker. The pepperoni fat renders into the broth and the beans absorb all the spices.
  • Pepperoni Is the Secret Don't skip the pepperoni — it adds a subtle smoky, savory depth that sets this chili apart from standard recipes. Slice it into quarters if the pieces are large so it distributes evenly.
  • Even Better the Next Day Like most chilis, Mom's Chili tastes significantly better reheated the next day once the flavors have had time to meld overnight in the refrigerator.
  • Drain the Beans Drain and rinse the kidney beans before adding them to reduce excess sodium and prevent the chili from becoming too thick or starchy.
