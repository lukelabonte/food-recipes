Delicious Ham and Potato Soup

A cozy, creamy comfort bowl that comes together in under an hour.

Overview

  • Prep: 20 min
  • Cook: 25 min
  • Total: 45 min
  • Servings: 8
  • Cooking Method: Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 cups peeled and diced potatoes (525 g)
  • 1/3 cup diced celery (50 g)
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped onion (53 g)
  • 3/4 cup diced cooked ham (113 g)
  • 3 1/4 cups water (770 g)
  • 2 tablespoons chicken bouillon granules (18 g)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (3 g)
  • 1 teaspoon ground white or black pepper (2 g)
  • 5 tablespoons butter (71 g)
  • 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour (39 g)
  • 2 cups milk (488 g)

Steps

  1. Combine the potatoes, celery, onion, ham and water in a stockpot. Bring to a boil, then cook over medium heat until potatoes are tender, about 10 to 15 minutes. Stir in the chicken bouillon, salt and pepper.
  2. In a separate saucepan, melt butter over medium-low heat. Whisk in flour with a fork, and cook, stirring constantly until thick, about 1 minute. Slowly stir in milk as not to allow lumps to form until all of the milk has been added. Continue stirring over medium-low heat until thick, 4 to 5 minutes.
  3. Stir the milk mixture into the stockpot, and cook soup until heated through. Serve immediately.
