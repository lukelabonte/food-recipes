Mom's Potato Salad

A classic, creamy potato salad made with tangy salad dressing, mustard, and sweet relish, topped with sliced hard-boiled eggs and a dusting of paprika.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Chill 1 hr recommended
  • Total 1 hr 30 min, including recommended chilling
  • Servings 8
  • Method Stovetop / No-Bake Assembly

Ingredients

  • 4 cans (15 oz each) sliced potatoes, drained (~1,700 g)
    • Fresh boiled Yukon Gold potatoes, diced — 1:1 by weight Yields a firmer, earthier texture with slightly more flavor; very similar calorie profile
    • Fresh boiled red potatoes, diced — 1:1 by weight Holds shape better and has a slightly waxy texture; nearly identical calories
  • 5 large eggs (~250 g)
  • 1 cup salad dressing (such as Miracle Whip) (~225 g)
    • Light Miracle Whip — 1:1 Lower calorie option with the same tangy flavor profile; slightly thinner consistency
    • Greek yogurt blended with 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp vinegar — 1:1 Noticeably lower calorie and higher protein; tangier and less sweet than the original
  • 1 tablespoon yellow mustard (~15 g)
    • Dijon mustard — 1:1 Adds a slightly more complex, sharper flavor; essentially the same calories
  • 1/4 cup sweet pickle relish (~60 g)
    • Dill pickle relish — 1:1 Less sweet and more savory; slightly lower calorie; changes the flavor profile noticeably
    • Finely diced dill pickles — 1:1 Chunkier texture, tangier taste; lower calorie than sweet relish
  • Salt, to taste (~3 g)
  • Black pepper, to taste (~1 g)
  • Paprika, for topping (~2 g)
    • Smoked paprika — 1:1 Adds a subtle smoky depth; same calorie level, visually similar

Steps

  1. Drain the canned sliced potatoes thoroughly and place them in a large pot. Cover with water and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook until fork-tender, about 8–10 minutes. Drain and set aside to cool slightly.
  2. Meanwhile, place the 5 eggs in a separate saucepan and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 8–10 minutes until hard-boiled. Drain and immediately cover the eggs with cold water to stop cooking. Let sit for 5 minutes before peeling.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the salad dressing, yellow mustard, sweet pickle relish, salt, and pepper. Stir until well blended.
  4. Add the drained, cooled potatoes to the dressing mixture. Peel and slice 4 of the hard-boiled eggs and fold them into the potato mixture. Stir gently to combine everything without breaking up the potatoes too much.
  5. Transfer the potato salad to a casserole dish or large serving bowl and smooth the top with a spatula.
  6. Peel and slice the remaining hard-boiled egg and arrange the slices decoratively across the top of the salad.
  7. Sprinkle lightly with paprika for color and a touch of flavor. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving for best flavor.

Tips

  • Chill Before Serving Potato salad tastes significantly better after resting in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour, or ideally overnight. The flavors meld and the dressing thickens slightly as it chills.
  • Don't Skip the Cold Water Bath for Eggs Covering the boiled eggs immediately with cold water stops the cooking process and prevents the grey-green ring from forming around the yolk, keeping them bright yellow and appetizing.
  • Salad Dressing vs. Mayonnaise Miracle Whip or similar salad dressings are sweeter and tangier than plain mayonnaise. This recipe specifically calls for salad dressing, not mayo, which is key to the classic flavor of this dish.
  • Season After Mixing Potatoes absorb salt as they sit, so taste the salad after mixing and again after chilling before serving. You may want to adjust salt and pepper before bringing it to the table.
