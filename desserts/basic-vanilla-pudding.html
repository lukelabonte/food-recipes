Basic Vanilla Pudding

Creamy, old-fashioned stovetop pudding made from scratch. Top it however you like.

Overview Ingredients Steps Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Total 25 min
  • Servings 4
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 3 cups milk (732 g)
    • Oat milk — 1:1 Dairy-free; slightly thinner, add 1 tbsp extra flour
    • Coconut milk, full-fat — 1:1 Richer; adds subtle coconut flavor, higher calorie
  • 2 egg yolks (34 g)
  • ½ cup sugar (96 g)
  • ⅓ cup flour (42 g)
    • Cornstarch — 2½ tbsp Silkier texture; clearer appearance; gluten-free
  • ¼ tsp. salt (1.5 g)
  • 2 tsp. vanilla (8.5 g)

Steps

  1. In a medium saucepan, bring 2½ cups of the milk to a boil over medium heat.
  2. While the milk heats, whisk together the remaining ½ cup milk, egg yolks, sugar, and flour in a bowl until a smooth paste forms.
  3. Once the milk is boiling, slowly whisk in the paste. Reduce heat to low and continue boiling, whisking constantly, until the pudding is thick, about 5 to 8 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat. Stir in the salt and vanilla.
  5. Pour into serving dishes and let cool before topping and serving.
