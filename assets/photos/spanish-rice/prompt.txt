Two wide white ceramic bowls filled with a hearty Spanish rice dish — tomato-red sauced rice flecked with crumbled browned ground beef, pale chunks of tender green pepper, and bits of softened onion. The mixture is glossy and saucy, with the rice grains coated in a rich rust-orange tomato sauce. Silver forks rest alongside the bowls on a layered linen napkin with subtle purple stripe accents.
