Four bone-in skin-on chicken thighs in an air fryer basket, viewed from directly above. The skin is deeply caramelized with a glossy reddish-orange gochujang glaze and patches of dark charring where the brown sugar has blackened. The edges are crispy and curled, with a lacquered sheen from the sesame and olive oil. Rendered juices and fat pool at the bottom of the basket around the thighs. The color ranges from bright tangerine-orange on the glazed surfaces to deep mahogany-black on the charred spots.
