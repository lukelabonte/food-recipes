Gochujang Chicken

Crispy air fryer chicken thighs with a sweet-spicy gochujang glaze that caramelizes into something dangerously good.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Total 25 min
  • Servings 4
  • Method Air Fryer

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar (14 g)
  • 1 tablespoon gochujang (18 g)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (14 g)
    • Avocado oil — 1:1 Higher smoke point; neutral flavor; similar calories
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder (5 g)
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper (2 g)
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (5 g)
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder (6 g)
  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, patted dry (1,040 g)
  • ½ teaspoon salt (3 g)

Steps

  1. Pat the chicken thighs very dry with paper towels on all sides. This is key for crispy skin.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the gochujang, sesame oil, olive oil, brown sugar, baking powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Mix into a paste.
  3. Rub the paste evenly over all sides of the chicken thighs.
  4. Place the thighs skin-side down in the air fryer basket. Cook at 380°F for 10 minutes.
  5. Flip the thighs skin-side up and air fry for another 6 to 10 minutes, until the skin is crispy and golden and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  6. Rest for 2 to 3 minutes before serving.

Tips

  • The baking powder helps the skin crisp up in the air fryer. Don't skip it.
  • Don't overcrowd the basket. The thighs need space for airflow on all sides.
  • If you don't have an air fryer, roast at 425°F on a wire rack for 35 to 40 minutes, flipping halfway.
