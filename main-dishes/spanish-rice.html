Spanish Rice

A hearty one-dish meal of seasoned ground beef, tender rice, and vegetables simmered in a rich tomato sauce with a gentle chili kick. Simple to make and deeply satisfying.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 35 min
  • Total 45 min
  • Servings 4
  • Method Stovetop + Oven

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup thinly sliced onion (75 g)
    • Shallots — 1:1 Milder and slightly sweeter than yellow onion; works well without changing the dish character.
  • 1/2 cup diced green pepper (75 g)
    • Red or yellow bell pepper — 1:1 Sweeter flavor profile; adds a bit more color but maintains the vegetable bulk.
  • 2 cups Minute Rice (instant white rice) (190 g)
    • Instant brown rice — 1:1 Slightly nuttier flavor and chewier texture; adds fiber and is slightly lower calorie density per cup cooked.
  • 1/4 cup butter (57 g)
    • Olive oil — 1:1 Lower in saturated fat; adds a subtle fruity note but preserves the sauté function well.
  • 2 cups hot water (473 g)
    • Beef broth — 1:1 Adds deeper savory flavor to the rice; slightly higher sodium, so reduce added salt accordingly.
  • 2 small cans tomato sauce (425 g)
    • Crushed tomatoes — 1:1 Chunkier texture; adds body and a slightly more rustic character to the dish.
  • 1 teaspoon salt (6 g)
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper (1 g)
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder (3 g)
    • Smoked paprika — 1:1 Less heat, more smoky depth; great if serving to those sensitive to spice.
  • 1 pound ground beef (80/20) (454 g)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a stovetop-safe casserole dish or Dutch oven over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the sliced onion, diced green pepper, instant rice, and crumbled ground beef. Stir to combine, cover, and sauté for approximately 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beef is mostly browned and the vegetables begin to soften.
  3. Carefully drain off the excess grease and butter from the casserole dish.
  4. Add the hot water, tomato sauce, salt, black pepper, and chili powder to the casserole dish. Stir everything together until well combined, then cover the dish tightly with a lid or foil.
  5. Transfer the covered casserole dish to the preheated oven and bake for 30 minutes, until the rice has absorbed the liquid and the mixture is bubbly and cooked through. Serve hot directly from the dish.

Tips

  • Drain Thoroughly Draining off the grease after browning is important for both flavor and texture — excess fat can make the rice greasy and prevent it from absorbing the tomato sauce properly.
  • Seal Tightly for Steaming Make sure your casserole lid fits snugly, or cover tightly with foil before baking. Instant rice needs trapped steam to cook fully and absorb all the liquid.
  • Lean Beef Option Using 90/10 or 93/7 lean ground beef reduces the amount of grease to drain and makes the final dish lighter without sacrificing protein.
  • Spice to Taste This recipe is mildly spiced. For more heat, add a pinch of cayenne or a dash of hot sauce with the other seasonings before baking.
