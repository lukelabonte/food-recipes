Matt's Beer Brats

Bratwursts simmered in a sweet and savory beer bath with onions and dark brown sugar, then served in toasted buns with sauerkraut, mustard, and sliced onions.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Total 40 min
  • Servings 8
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 1–2 packages bratwurst (about 5 brats per package) (900 g)
  • 4–6 cans beer (12 oz each) (1,800 g)
    • Non-alcoholic beer — 1:1 Keeps the malty flavor with no alcohol
    • Apple cider — 1:1 Sweeter, fruitier flavor and no alcohol
  • 1/2 onion, sliced (75 g)
  • 1 cup dark brown sugar (220 g)
    • Light brown sugar — 1:1 Milder molasses flavor
    • Coconut sugar — 1:1 Similar caramel notes, slightly less sweet
  • 8 bratwurst buns (~400 g)
  • Sauerkraut, for serving (optional)
  • Mustard, for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Pour the beer into a large pot or deep pan and bring it to a low boil over medium heat.
  2. Add the sliced onion and dark brown sugar to the beer and stir until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Add the brats to the pot, making sure they are mostly submerged. Reduce to a gentle simmer and cook until the brats are cooked through and firm, about 20 to 30 minutes.
  4. Serve the brats in buns topped with sauerkraut, mustard, or beer-simmered onions.

Tips

  • Choose your beer wisely Light beer gives a mild taste, while darker beers leave a strong beer flavor in the brats.
  • Finish on the grill For nicer color and char marks, move the simmered brats to a hot grill or skillet for a few minutes before serving.
  • Keep it at a simmer After adding the brats, lower the heat to a gentle simmer. A hard boil can split the casings and dry out the brats.
