Chicken Breast Casserole

A comforting, weeknight-friendly casserole layered with tender chicken breasts, creamy mushroom sauce, and a golden, herb-seasoned stuffing crust that bakes up perfectly in under an hour.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Total 50 min
  • Servings 4
  • Method Oven

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 6 oz each) (680 g)
  • 1/3 cup sour cream (80 g)
    • Plain Greek yogurt — 1:1 Lower calorie option; adds slight tang and extra protein. Texture stays creamy but may be slightly thinner.
    • Light sour cream — 1:1 Lower calorie than full-fat sour cream; flavor and texture are nearly identical.
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup (298 g)
    • Cream of chicken soup — 1:1 Very similar consistency and richness; shifts flavor slightly away from mushroom but preserves the creamy casserole character.
    • Low-sodium cream of mushroom soup — 1:1 Reduces sodium significantly; flavor is nearly identical, same calorie level.
  • 1 package (6 oz) Stove Top stuffing mix (chicken flavor) (170 g)
    • Homemade herb breadcrumbs with butter and chicken broth — 1:1 by volume Cleaner ingredient option; requires mixing dried bread cubes with butter and broth before topping. Slightly lower sodium.
  • 1/4 cup milk, approximately, adjusting as needed to moisten stuffing (60 g)
    • Chicken broth — 1:1 Adds savory depth in place of milk; lower calorie and dairy-free. Slightly saltier result.
    • Unsweetened oat milk or almond milk — 1:1 Dairy-free alternative; neutral flavor works well, slightly lower calorie than whole milk.

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (205°C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray or butter to prevent sticking.
  2. Arrange the 4 chicken breasts in a single layer in the prepared baking dish, spacing them evenly so they cook uniformly.
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the sour cream and the full can of cream of mushroom soup until smooth and well combined. Pour this mixture evenly over the chicken breasts, making sure each piece is well coated.
  4. Sprinkle the dry Stove Top stuffing mix evenly over the top of the soup mixture. Drizzle or splash milk lightly over the stuffing — just enough to slightly moisten the topping so it bakes into a cohesive crust rather than remaining dusty and dry. Start with the amount listed in Ingredients and add more if needed.
  5. Bake uncovered at 400°F for 35–40 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F and the stuffing topping is golden brown and crisp. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Tips

  • Don't Over-Soak the Stuffing Add milk sparingly — just enough to lightly dampen the stuffing topping. Too much liquid will make it soggy rather than forming a crisp, golden crust.
  • Use a Meat Thermometer Chicken breast thickness varies widely. Always check that the thickest part of each breast reads 165°F internally to ensure safe doneness without over-drying thinner pieces.
  • Pound Chicken to Even Thickness If your chicken breasts are particularly thick, pound them lightly to an even 3/4-inch thickness before baking. This ensures they cook at the same rate and stay juicy.
  • Cover Then Uncover for Best Results For extra-juicy chicken, cover the dish with foil for the first 25 minutes, then uncover for the remaining 15 minutes to crisp up the stuffing topping.
