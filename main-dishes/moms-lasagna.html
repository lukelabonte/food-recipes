Mom's Lasagna

A hearty, comforting classic layered with tender noodles, creamy cottage cheese, rich tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella — just like Grandma used to make.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 1 hr
  • Total 1 hr 20 min
  • Servings 10
  • Method Oven

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 large cans (about 52 oz total) garlic herb spaghetti sauce (~1,475 g)
    • Homemade marinara — 1:1 Lower sodium and no added sugar; richer, fresher tomato flavor with slightly more effort
    • Plain tomato sauce with Italian seasoning — 1:1 Milder flavor; add garlic powder, basil, and oregano to compensate; lower calorie
  • 1 large container (32 oz) cottage cheese (~907 g)
    • Whole-milk ricotta — 1:1 Richer, creamier texture and slightly higher calorie; more traditional Italian-style lasagna
    • Part-skim ricotta — 1:1 Similar creamy texture to cottage cheese; lower calorie than whole-milk ricotta
  • 1 box (16 oz) lasagna noodles (~454 g)
    • No-boil lasagna noodles — 1:1 Saves time; add a splash of water to sauce layers to ensure noodles fully hydrate during baking
    • Gluten-free lasagna noodles — 1:1 Suitable for gluten intolerance; texture may be slightly softer; same calorie range
  • 2 packages (16 oz each) shredded mozzarella cheese (~907 g)
    • Part-skim shredded mozzarella — 1:1 Lower calorie and lower fat; melts similarly with a slightly less rich flavor
    • Provolone (shredded) — 1:1 Sharper, slightly smoky flavor; melts well; similar calorie level
  • 1 large egg (~50 g)
    • 1 tablespoon plain Greek yogurt — 1:1 (per egg) Binds the cottage cheese mixture similarly; negligible flavor difference; slightly lower calorie

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (205°C). Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the lasagna noodles until just tender according to package directions. Do not drain the pot — leave the noodles submerged in the hot water and remove individual noodles with tongs as you assemble the lasagna. This prevents sticking.
  2. Spread a thin, even layer of garlic herb spaghetti sauce across the bottom of a 9x13-inch casserole dish. This prevents the bottom noodles from sticking and drying out during baking.
  3. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the cottage cheese and egg. Stir well until fully incorporated into a cohesive, creamy mixture.
  4. Lay a single layer of noodles over the sauce in the casserole dish, overlapping slightly as needed to cover the surface. Dot generous spoonfuls of the cottage cheese mixture evenly over the noodles. Ladle spaghetti sauce over the cottage cheese layer, then sprinkle a generous handful of shredded mozzarella on top.
  5. Continue repeating the layers — noodles, cottage cheese mixture, sauce, mozzarella — until you reach about 1/4 inch from the top of the dish. Finish the top layer with a final spread of sauce and a generous covering of shredded mozzarella cheese.
  6. Cover the dish tightly with nonstick aluminum foil, ensuring the foil does not touch the cheese on top. Bake at 400°F for 1 hour, until the sauce is bubbling around the edges and the cheese is fully melted. For a golden, bubbly top, remove the foil during the last 10 minutes of baking. Let rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.

Tips

  • Don't Drain the Noodles Keeping the boiled noodles submerged in the pot of hot water throughout assembly is the key trick here — it keeps them pliable and prevents them from sticking together or tearing when you lift them out with tongs.
  • Let It Rest Before Cutting Allowing the lasagna to sit for at least 10–15 minutes after coming out of the oven lets the layers firm up, making it far easier to cut clean, structured slices rather than a saucy mess.
  • Foil Tent for a Golden Top If you want a browned, slightly crispy cheese top, remove the foil for the last 10 minutes of baking. Keep it covered the whole time for a softer, saucier finish.
  • Make It Ahead This lasagna assembles beautifully up to 24 hours in advance. Cover and refrigerate unbaked, then bake directly from the fridge — add 15–20 extra minutes to the bake time to ensure it heats through completely.
  • Season the Cottage Cheese For extra flavor, add a pinch of salt, black pepper, and dried Italian seasoning or fresh chopped basil to the cottage cheese and egg mixture before layering.
