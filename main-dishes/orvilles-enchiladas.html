Orville's Enchiladas

Tender corn tortillas stuffed with savory meat and melted Monterey Jack, blanketed in rich chili con queso and baked until golden and bubbling.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Total 45 min
  • Servings 6
  • Method Oven

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas (300 g)
    • Flour tortillas — 1:1 Softer texture and slightly higher calorie; less authentic corn flavor but easier to roll without cracking.
  • 2 cups prepared enchilada filling (seasoned ground beef, shredded pork, or shredded chicken) (400 g)
    • Black beans and roasted corn — 1:1 Lower calorie vegetarian option; maintains heartiness but changes the flavor profile significantly.
  • 1 1/2 cups grated Monterey Jack cheese (170 g)
    • Pepper Jack cheese — 1:1 Same melt quality with added heat; slightly spicier flavor that complements the chili con queso.
    • Colby-Jack blend — 1:1 Milder flavor, melts similarly; widely available and a natural complement to the dish.
  • 1 1/2 cups chili con queso (360 g)
    • Homemade green chile white sauce — 1:1 Fresher flavor and lower calorie depending on recipe; changes color and heat profile slightly.
  • 2 tablespoons butter, for the tortillas (28 g)
    • Olive oil — 1:1 Lower saturated fat; slightly different flavor but achieves the same softening effect on the tortillas.
  • Cooking spray, for the baking dish (5 g)

Steps

  1. Prepare your enchilada filling of choice — seasoned and cooked ground beef, shredded pork, or shredded chicken. Season well with cumin, garlic, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Also prepare or open your chili con queso and keep it warm.
  2. Preheat your oven to 375°F. Lightly coat a 13-by-9-inch baking dish with cooking spray and set aside.
  3. Lightly butter one side of each corn tortilla. Working in batches, place the tortillas butter-side down on a hot griddle or skillet over medium heat for about 30 seconds per side, just until softened and pliable. Do not over-cook — you want them flexible, not crispy.
  4. Transfer each softened tortilla to a clean work surface. Spoon a portion of the enchilada filling down the center, then add a pinch of grated Monterey Jack cheese. Roll the tortilla tightly around the filling and place seam-side down in the prepared baking dish.
  5. Repeat the filling and rolling process with all the tortillas, arranging them snugly in a single layer in the baking dish.
  6. Sprinkle the remaining Monterey Jack cheese evenly over the top of the rolled enchiladas. Pour the chili con queso over the entire dish, spreading it to cover.
  7. Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake at 375°F for 25 minutes, until the cheese is fully melted, the filling is heated through, and the edges are bubbling.
  8. Remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes before serving. Serve hot alongside Spanish rice and refried beans.

Tips

  • Warm Your Tortillas Quickly If you're short on time, you can microwave the corn tortillas in a damp paper towel for 30–45 seconds to soften them instead of using a griddle. The butter step can be skipped in this case, though you'll lose a touch of flavor.
  • Don't Skip the Seam-Side Down Always place rolled enchiladas seam-side down in the baking dish. This prevents them from unrolling during baking and helps them hold their shape for serving.
  • Keep the Queso Pourable If your chili con queso is thick, warm it slightly before pouring so it spreads evenly over all the enchiladas. A cold, stiff queso won't distribute well and can result in uneven coverage.
  • Make It Ahead These enchiladas can be assembled a day in advance, covered tightly, and refrigerated. Add the chili con queso just before baking and increase baking time by 10 minutes to account for the cold start.
