Maria's Pepper Steak

A sweet and savory stir-fry with tender flank steak, bell peppers, and a honey-soy glaze that's hard to mess up.

Overview Ingredients Steps Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Total 45 min
  • Servings 6
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (27 g)
    • Avocado oil — 1:1 Higher smoke point; neutral flavor; similar calories
    • Vegetable oil — 1:1 Neutral flavor; similar calories
  • 2 large bell peppers, sliced into thin strips (300 g)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped (150 g)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (6 g)
  • ⅓ cup soy sauce (80 g)
    • Tamari — 1:1 Gluten-free; slightly richer flavor
    • Coconut aminos — 1:1 Lower sodium; slightly sweeter
  • ⅓ cup honey (113 g)
    • Maple syrup — 1:1 Slightly different flavor profile; similar calories
  • ⅓ cup red wine vinegar (79 g)
    • Apple cider vinegar — 1:1 Slightly fruitier; works well in stir-fries
  • 1½ pounds flank steak, cut into thin strips (680 g)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add bell peppers, onion, and garlic. Sauté until crisp-tender, 3 to 4 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.
  2. Increase the heat to medium-high. Pour soy sauce, honey, and vinegar into the skillet. Add beef and cook, stirring frequently, for 10 to 15 minutes.
  3. Return the cooked vegetables to the skillet and cook for 10 to 15 more minutes, until the sauce thickens and the beef is tender.
