Orville's Spanish Rice

A boldly seasoned, golden-toasted rice simmered with zesty Ro-Tel tomatoes, cumin, and chili powder — a simple, satisfying Tex-Mex classic that pairs with almost anything.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Total 35 min
  • Servings 6
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup olive oil (54 g)
    • Avocado oil — 1:1 Neutral flavor with a high smoke point; similar calorie level and works well for toasting the rice.
    • Vegetable oil — 1:1 Very neutral flavor; slightly lower quality fat profile but functionally identical for this recipe.
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice (278 g)
    • Long-grain brown rice — 1:1 Nuttier flavor and chewier texture; requires an additional 10–15 minutes of cooking time and slightly more liquid. Similar calorie level but higher in fiber.
    • Basmati rice — 1:1 Aromatic and fluffy; cooks in a similar timeframe and yields a slightly more fragrant dish at the same calorie level.
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt (9 g)
    • Kosher salt — 1.5:1 (use 2 1/4 tsp) Less dense than table salt; adjust upward to achieve the same level of seasoning. Lower sodium per teaspoon.
  • 1 can (10 oz) Ro-Tel diced tomatoes and green chiles (283 g)
    • Diced canned tomatoes + 1 small can diced green chiles — 1:1 combined Replicates the flavor profile of Ro-Tel; use mild or hot green chiles to control heat level.
  • 1 teaspoon cumin (3 g)
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder (1 g)
  • Water, approximately 2 1/4 cups (combined with tomato liquid to equal 3 cups total) (532 g)
    • Chicken broth — 1:1 Adds savory depth and richness to the rice; slightly higher in sodium, so reduce added salt accordingly.
    • Vegetable broth — 1:1 Keeps the dish vegetarian/vegan while adding more flavor than plain water. Watch sodium content.

Steps

  1. Heat 1/4 cup of olive oil in a medium heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium to medium-high heat until shimmering.
  2. Add the dry long-grain rice to the hot oil and cook, stirring constantly, until the grains turn a light golden brown, about 4–6 minutes. Watch carefully to avoid burning.
  3. Add the salt, cumin, and chili powder directly to the toasted rice and stir well to coat every grain with the spices, about 30 seconds.
  4. Drain the liquid from the Ro-Tel tomatoes into a measuring cup, then add enough water to bring the total liquid to exactly 3 cups. Add the liquid and the tomatoes to the rice and stir to combine.
  5. Increase the heat to medium-high and bring the mixture to a full boil, stirring once.
  6. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, place the lid firmly on the saucepan, and cook for 20 minutes. Do not lift the lid during this time — the trapped steam is essential for properly cooked rice.
  7. After 20 minutes, remove from heat and let sit, still covered, for 5 minutes. Then remove the lid and stir gently to distribute the tomatoes and fluff the rice before serving.

Tips

  • Toast the Rice Properly Don't rush the browning step — toasting the rice in oil until golden is what gives Spanish rice its signature nutty, non-mushy texture. Stir constantly to ensure even browning without scorching.
  • Don't Peek While It Cooks Lifting the lid during the 20-minute steam releases critical moisture and disrupts the cooking process, leading to unevenly cooked or crunchy rice. Trust the timer.
  • Liquid Ratio is Key The Ro-Tel tomatoes contribute liquid, so combining that drained juice with water to reach exactly 3 cups ensures the correct 2:1 liquid-to-rice ratio for perfectly cooked long-grain rice.
  • Customize the Heat Level Ro-Tel comes in mild, original, and hot varieties. Choose based on your heat preference, or add a pinch of cayenne along with the chili powder for extra kick.
  • Make It a Meal Stir in a can of drained black beans and frozen corn during the last 5 minutes of cooking to turn this side dish into a hearty vegetarian main.
