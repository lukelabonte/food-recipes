Mom's BBQ Beans

A sweet and smoky baked bean casserole with a rich, caramelized glaze that's the perfect crowd-pleasing side dish for any cookout or family gathering.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 60 min
  • Total 65 min
  • Servings 8
  • Method Oven

Ingredients

  • 3 cans (15 oz each) Campbell's Pork and Beans (1,275 g)
    • Navy beans or Great Northern beans (cooked) — 1:1 by volume Slightly less saucy and less seasoned than canned pork and beans; add a splash of molasses and a pinch of salt to compensate. Slightly lower calorie.
  • 1/4 cup chopped onions (40 g)
    • Shallots — 1:1 Milder and slightly sweeter than white onion; blends seamlessly into the sauce during baking.
    • Dried minced onion — 1 tbsp per 1/4 cup of listed chopped onion (rehydrated) Convenient pantry swap with a softer texture; flavor is slightly less sharp.
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce (hickory smoked recommended) (240 g)
    • Homemade BBQ sauce — 1:1 Allows full control of sweetness and smoke level; can be made lower calorie by reducing sugar content.
  • 1 cup brown sugar (200 g)
    • Coconut sugar — 1:1 Similar caramel-like depth with a slightly lower glycemic index; nearly identical calorie content but less refined.
    • Honey — 3/4 cup per 1 cup of listed brown sugar Adds floral sweetness and a glossier glaze; slightly higher calorie and will make the dish more liquid initially.

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (205°C) and set a rack in the center position.
  2. In a large casserole dish, combine all three cans of pork and beans, the chopped onions, BBQ sauce, and brown sugar. Stir everything together thoroughly until the sugar is dissolved and the mixture is uniform.
  3. Cover the casserole dish with a lid or aluminum foil and bake for 45 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the beans to heat through completely.
  4. Remove the cover and continue baking for an additional 15 minutes, allowing the top to caramelize and develop a rich, bubbly glaze. Remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Tips

  • Hickory Smoked BBQ Sauce Is Key The recipe specifically calls for hickory smoked BBQ sauce, and for good reason — the smoky depth balances the sweetness of the brown sugar and creates that classic backyard BBQ flavor. Sweet Baby Ray's Hickory & Brown Sugar or a similar variety works beautifully here.
  • Don't Skip the Uncovered Bake The final 15 minutes uncovered is what creates the signature caramelized, jammy top layer. Resist the urge to skip this step — it transforms the dish from soupy to saucy and sticky.
  • Make It Ahead This dish reheats exceptionally well. You can bake it a day ahead, refrigerate it covered, and reheat at 325°F for 20–25 minutes. The flavors actually deepen overnight.
  • Customize the Heat For a spicier version, stir in 1–2 teaspoons of hot sauce or a pinch of cayenne pepper before baking. It balances the sweetness and adds complexity without overpowering the dish.
