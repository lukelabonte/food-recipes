Simple Garlic Bread

Crispy, golden baguette slathered with fragrant garlic butter — a classic side that comes together in minutes and pairs perfectly with pasta, soup, or salad.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Total 15 min
  • Servings 4
  • Method Oven

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, sliced in half lengthwise (~250 g)
    • Ciabatta loaf — 1:1 by weight Slightly chewier crumb with a more open texture; absorbs butter well and delivers a similar golden crust.
    • Italian bread loaf — 1:1 by weight Softer interior than a baguette; slightly lower calorie depending on brand, with a mild flavor that works well with garlic butter.
  • 4 tablespoons softened unsalted butter (~57 g)
    • Salted butter — 1:1 Works seamlessly; simply omit the added pinch of salt to avoid over-salting.
    • Vegan margarine (plant-based butter) — 1:1 Keeps the dish dairy-free with a very similar flavor and texture; calorie content is roughly comparable.
    • Olive oil — 1:1 by volume Slightly lower in saturated fat; produces a crispier, more rustic result with a fruitier flavor note. Brush on rather than spread.
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (~9 g)
    • Garlic powder — ¾ teaspoon per 3 cloves Convenient pantry swap with a mellower, slightly sweeter garlic flavor; mixes smoothly into the butter with no raw bite.
  • 1 pinch of salt (~1 g)
    • Flaky sea salt — 1:1 pinch Adds a subtle crunch and slightly more complex mineral flavor when sprinkled on top just before baking.

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a baking sheet with foil for easy cleanup.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the softened butter, minced garlic, and a pinch of salt. Mix thoroughly until the garlic is evenly distributed throughout the butter.
  3. Slice the baguette in half lengthwise so you have two long flat pieces. Place them cut-side up on the prepared baking sheet.
  4. Spread the garlic butter mixture evenly over the cut surface of both baguette halves, making sure to cover all the way to the edges so the crust gets golden and crisp.
  5. Bake for 10 minutes, or until the bread is golden brown and the edges are lightly crisp. For extra color, switch the oven to broil for the final 1–2 minutes, watching closely to prevent burning.
  6. Remove from the oven and let rest for 1–2 minutes. Slice each half into 4 pieces (yielding 8 pieces total) and serve immediately while warm.

Tips

  • Soften Butter Quickly If you forgot to take the butter out ahead of time, cut it into small cubes and let it sit at room temperature for 10 minutes, or microwave in 5-second bursts until just soft but not melted.
  • Go Fresh for Best Flavor Freshly minced garlic gives the most pungent, aromatic result. For a milder, sweeter garlic flavor, use a garlic press or roast the cloves beforehand.
  • Broil for a Crispier Finish After the initial bake, a quick 1–2 minutes under the broiler adds deep golden color and a satisfying crunch. Keep a close eye on it — garlic bread can go from golden to burnt very quickly under the broiler.
  • Make It Ahead You can assemble the buttered bread, wrap it tightly in foil, and refrigerate it for up to 24 hours before baking. Bake straight from the fridge — just add 2–3 extra minutes to the cook time.
