Rotel Tomato Cheese Dip

The ultimate two-ingredient queso dip — creamy, velvety Velveeta melted together with zesty Ro-Tel tomatoes and green chiles for a crowd-pleasing snack that comes together in minutes.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 2 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Total 7 min
  • Servings 8
  • Method Microwave

Ingredients

  • 1 can (10 oz) Ro-Tel Original Diced Tomatoes with Green Chiles, drained (283 g (before draining, approx. 200 g drained))
    • Canned diced tomatoes + 1 (4 oz) can diced green chiles — 1:1 combined Mimics Ro-Tel closely; slightly milder heat, nearly identical flavor profile and calorie count
    • Fresh pico de gallo, drained — 1:1 Brighter, fresher flavor with more texture; slightly lower calorie and sodium
  • 1 medium package (16 oz) Velveeta cheese, cubed (454 g)

Steps

  1. Cut the Velveeta block into 1-inch cubes and place them in a large microwave-safe casserole dish with a lid or microwave-safe cover. This helps the cheese melt evenly and prevents splattering.
  2. Microwave the Velveeta on high in 60-second intervals, stirring thoroughly between each interval, until the cheese is fully melted and smooth. This typically takes 3 to 4 minutes total depending on your microwave's wattage.
  3. Drain the can of Ro-Tel tomatoes well using a fine mesh strainer or the can lid, then stir the drained tomatoes and chiles into the melted Velveeta until evenly combined.
  4. Return the dish to the microwave and heat for 1 additional minute on high, then stir once more. Serve immediately in the dish or transfer to a slow cooker set to warm to keep the dip at the perfect consistency for a party.

Tips

  • Don't Skip Draining Draining the Ro-Tel tomatoes is essential — adding them with their liquid will thin out the dip and make it watery rather than thick and creamy.
  • Keep It Warm Velveeta dip firms up quickly as it cools. Transfer to a small slow cooker or fondue pot set on low to keep it perfectly dippable throughout a party.
  • Heat Level Use Ro-Tel Mild for a crowd-friendly dip, Ro-Tel Original for moderate kick, or Ro-Tel Hot for serious heat lovers. The heat level is entirely controlled by which variety you choose.
  • Stir Frequently When Microwaving Velveeta can scorch on the edges if left unattended. Stirring every 60 seconds ensures smooth, even melting without any burned spots.
  • Add-In Ideas Stir in cooked and crumbled breakfast sausage, browned ground beef seasoned with taco spice, or a handful of pickled jalapeños to upgrade the dip into a heartier queso.
