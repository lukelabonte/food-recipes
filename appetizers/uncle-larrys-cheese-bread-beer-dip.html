Uncle Larry's Cheese Bread Beer Dip

A crowd-pleasing party classic featuring a creamy, beer-spiked cheese dip poured into a hollowed-out rye bread bowl, surrounded by chunks of soft bread for dipping.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 0 min
  • Total 10 min
  • Servings 10
  • Method No-Cook / Assembly

Ingredients

  • 1 round rye bread loaf (2 pounds) (907 g)
    • Sourdough boule — 1:1 Slightly tangier flavor, less earthy than rye; holds up equally well as a bread bowl. Lower fiber content.
    • Pumpernickel round — 1:1 Darker, more robust flavor that complements the cheese dip; sturdier crust for transport.
  • 1 large jar Cheez Whiz (15 oz) (425 g)
  • 1 large package cream cheese (8 oz) (227 g)
    • Neufchâtel cheese — 1:1 Lower calorie and lower fat alternative with a nearly identical flavor and texture; blends just as smoothly.
  • 7 ounces beer (207 g)
    • Non-alcoholic beer — 1:1 Preserves the malty flavor profile without alcohol; slight difference in depth of flavor.
    • Chicken or vegetable broth — 1:1 Removes the beer character but keeps the dip fluid and savory; slightly lower calorie.
  • 1 teaspoon garlic salt (6 g)
    • Kosher salt and garlic powder: equal parts, totaling the listed garlic salt volume Allows independent control of salt and garlic levels; same overall flavor profile.

Steps

  1. Place the round rye bread loaf on a cutting board. Using a serrated knife, cut a circle into the top of the loaf and carefully hollow out the center, leaving the bottom and sides at least 1 inch thick to prevent the dip from leaking through.
  2. Tear or cut the removed bread into bite-sized chunks and arrange them around the outside of the hollowed bread bowl on a serving tray or in a wicker basket.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the Cheez Whiz, cream cheese, beer, and garlic salt. Using a hand mixer or a sturdy spatula, blend everything together until completely smooth and well combined.
  4. Pour the blended cheese dip into the hollowed-out bread bowl. Serve immediately with the surrounding bread chunks for dipping. If transporting to a party, carry the dip in a separate airtight container and fill the bread bowl after arriving at the destination.

Tips

  • Soften the Cream Cheese First Let the cream cheese sit at room temperature for 30 minutes before blending. This ensures a completely lump-free, silky-smooth dip without over-mixing.
  • Beer Choice Matters A mild lager (like Stella Artois, as pictured) keeps the flavor light and crowd-friendly. A darker ale or stout will add a richer, more robust flavor — great for beer lovers.
  • Transport Tip Never fill the bread bowl before transporting — the moisture will make the bread soggy and it may leak. Bring the dip in a separate container and assemble on-site.
  • Keep the Dip Warm If you want to serve this warm, gently heat the blended dip in a saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly, before pouring into the bread bowl. It stays creamy and pourable much longer when warm.
  • Extra Dippers Once the bread chunks are gone, have crackers, pretzels, celery sticks, or carrot sticks on hand as backup dippers — the cheese dip is the star of the show.
