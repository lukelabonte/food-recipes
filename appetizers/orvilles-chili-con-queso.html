Orville's Chili Con Queso

A rich, creamy cheese dip loaded with green chiles and warm spices — perfect for scooping with tortilla chips or drizzling over enchiladas.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Total 15 min
  • Servings 6
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces Velveeta cheese, cubed (227 g)
    • American cheese — 1:1 Melts similarly smooth; slightly less processed flavor but maintains the creamy queso texture
  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened (113 g)
    • Neufchâtel cheese — 1:1 Lower calorie option with slightly less fat; texture and flavor are nearly identical in a cooked dip
  • 3 tablespoons butter (43 g)
    • Unsalted butter — 1:1 Use unsalted and adjust seasoning to taste; no meaningful difference in texture
  • 1 can (4.5 ounces) chopped green chiles, undrained (128 g)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin (2 g)
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder (1 g)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup whole milk (122 g)
    • Half-and-half — 1:1 Richer and creamier result; slightly higher calorie
    • Evaporated milk — 1:1 Adds extra creaminess and helps prevent separation when reheated
  • Tortilla chips, for serving

Steps

  1. Cube the Velveeta and soften the cream cheese to help them melt evenly. Gather all remaining ingredients before starting.
  2. Combine the Velveeta, cream cheese, butter, chopped green chiles (with their liquid), cumin, chili powder, salt, pepper, and milk in a medium saucepan.
  3. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until all the cheeses have fully melted and the dip is smooth and creamy, about 8–10 minutes. Reduce heat to low if the mixture begins to bubble aggressively.
  4. Taste and adjust seasoning with additional salt, pepper, or chili powder as desired. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, or spoon over enchiladas as a sauce.

Tips

  • Keep It Warm Transfer the finished queso to a small slow cooker set on 'Warm' to keep it perfectly dippable throughout a party without it thickening or forming a skin.
  • Stir Constantly Stir the mixture frequently while melting to prevent the cheeses from scorching on the bottom of the pan, especially once they start to liquify.
  • Customize the Heat For extra kick, add a diced jalapeño or a pinch of cayenne pepper. For a smokier flavor, swap regular chili powder for chipotle chili powder.
  • Reheat Gently Leftovers can be reheated in a saucepan over low heat with a splash of milk stirred in to restore the creamy consistency.
