Bacon-Apple-Jalapeño Pop 'Ems

Crispy bacon-wrapped jalapeño halves stuffed with a creamy apple-scallion filling — the ultimate crowd-pleasing party bite that balances heat, tang, and smoky richness in every pop.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Total 65 min
  • Servings 40
  • Method Oven

Ingredients

  • 1 (8-ounce) package light cream cheese, at room temperature (227 g)
    • Neufchâtel cheese — 1:1 Very similar texture and tang with slightly lower calorie content; nearly indistinguishable in the final dish.
    • Regular full-fat cream cheese — 1:1 Richer and slightly higher calorie; produces a creamier, more indulgent filling.
  • 1 large Granny Smith apple, cored and minced (182 g)
    • Honeycrisp apple — 1:1 Sweeter and slightly less tart than Granny Smith; adds more sweetness but preserves the fruity contrast with the heat.
  • 3 large scallions, roots removed, green and white parts finely minced (60 g)
    • Chives — 1:1 Milder onion flavor; lower calorie and works well but loses some sharpness from the white parts of scallion.
  • Kosher salt, to taste (3 g)
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste (2 g)
  • 20 jalapeños (500 g)
  • 20 strips thin-sliced bacon, cut in half (280 g)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil or parchment paper for easy cleanup and to catch bacon drippings.
  2. In a medium bowl, stir the light cream cheese until completely smooth. Add the minced Granny Smith apple and finely minced scallions. Season generously with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, then stir until the filling is evenly combined.
  3. Put on food-safe gloves to protect your skin from capsaicin. Halve all the jalapeños lengthwise through the stem. Using a small spoon or paring knife, carefully remove the inner membranes and all seeds from each half to control the heat level.
  4. Using a small spoon, fill each jalapeño half with the cream cheese mixture, mounding it slightly above the rim of the pepper so the filling is generous and visible.
  5. Wrap each stuffed jalapeño half snugly with one half-strip of bacon, encasing the filling and overlapping the ends underneath. Press a toothpick through the bacon and pepper to secure everything in place.
  6. Arrange the wrapped poppers seam-side down on the prepared baking sheet, spacing them slightly apart so air can circulate. Bake for approximately 30 minutes, until the bacon is deeply browned and crispy underneath and the filling is set and lightly golden.
  7. Remove from the oven and allow to cool for about 5 minutes before transferring to a serving platter. The toothpicks make them easy to grab and serve straight from the tray.

Tips

  • Wear Gloves Jalapeño capsaicin clings to skin even after washing. Always use disposable nitrile or latex gloves when halving and seeding peppers, and avoid touching your eyes throughout the process.
  • Control the Heat The membranes and seeds are where most of the heat lives. For a milder crowd, remove them completely. For spice lovers, leave a few seeds in a handful of the peppers and mark them with a second toothpick as a fun warning.
  • Thin Bacon Is Key Thin-sliced bacon crisps and cooks through fully in the same time it takes the filling to set. Thick-cut bacon risks being undercooked and chewy by the time the rest of the popper is done.
  • Make Ahead Friendly Assemble the poppers fully — filled and bacon-wrapped — up to 24 hours in advance. Cover and refrigerate on the baking sheet, then bake straight from the fridge, adding 3–5 minutes to the cook time.
  • Foil for Easy Cleanup Bacon renders a significant amount of fat. Lining your baking sheet with foil means you can discard the drippings without scrubbing — a small detail that makes a big difference after party cleanup.
