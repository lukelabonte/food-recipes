Glazed Meatballs

Tender meatballs simmered in a sweet-and-tangy chili-grape glaze that caramelizes into an irresistible sticky coating. A beloved party classic that comes together with just three ingredients.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Total 30 min
  • Servings 12
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 1 package (32 oz) frozen meatballs, thawed (907 g)
    • Turkey meatballs — 1:1 Lower calorie and leaner than beef meatballs; slightly lighter flavor but absorbs the glaze just as well.
    • Plant-based meatballs — 1:1 Lower calorie option; texture may be slightly softer and flavor more neutral, but the glaze carries the dish.
  • 2 jars (12 oz each) chili sauce (680 g)
    • Cocktail sauce — 1:1 Very similar flavor profile with a slightly stronger horseradish note; works seamlessly as a swap.
    • Ketchup mixed with hot sauce: 1:1 with the listed chili sauce; use 1 tsp hot sauce per 24 oz of listed chili sauce Lower calorie approximation; less complex but provides the tomato-sweet base needed for the glaze.
  • 1 large jar (18 oz) grape jelly (510 g)
    • Currant jelly — 1:1 Slightly more tart and less sweet than grape jelly; produces a more sophisticated, wine-like glaze.
    • Cranberry sauce (whole berry) — 1:1 Adds a festive tartness; results in a chunkier glaze with a more complex fruit flavor.

Steps

  1. Place a large kettle or heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the listed chili sauce and grape jelly. Stir continuously as the mixture heats, breaking up the jelly until it fully dissolves into the chili sauce and the two are completely combined, about 5–7 minutes.
  2. Add the thawed meatballs to the pot and stir gently to coat every meatball evenly with the glaze.
  3. Reduce heat to low, cover the pot, and cook for 18–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the meatballs are heated all the way through and the sauce has thickened into a glossy, sticky glaze. Serve directly from the pot or transfer to a slow cooker set to warm for a party.

Tips

  • Thaw Meatballs First Make sure meatballs are fully thawed before adding them to the sauce. Adding frozen meatballs will water down the glaze and significantly extend cooking time.
  • Slow Cooker Friendly This recipe translates perfectly to a slow cooker. Combine all ingredients and cook on LOW for 4 hours or HIGH for 2 hours. Keep on the WARM setting for serving at parties.
  • Use Jelly, Not Jam Grape jelly melts smoothly into the sauce, creating a silky glaze. Jam contains fruit pieces and pectin that can make the sauce uneven and overly thick.
  • Make It Spicier Add a pinch of crushed red pepper flakes or a dash of hot sauce to the glaze if you prefer a little heat to balance the sweetness of the jelly.
