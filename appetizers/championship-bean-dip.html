Championship Bean Dip

A rich, cheesy slow-cooker bean dip loaded with refried beans, two melted cheeses, and a kick of chili spice — the ultimate crowd-pleasing party appetizer.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 2 hr
  • Total 2 hr 10 min
  • Servings 9
  • Method Slow Cooker

Ingredients

  • 1 can (16 oz) refried beans (454 g)
    • Black bean refried beans — 1:1 Slightly earthier flavor with a darker color; similar texture and calorie level
    • Homemade mashed pinto beans — 1:1 Fresher flavor and lower sodium; slightly looser texture depending on preparation
  • 1 cup picante sauce (240 g)
    • Chunky salsa — 1:1 Chunkier texture in the finished dip; similar heat and flavor profile
    • Rotel diced tomatoes & green chiles — 1:1 Adds more tomato chunk and slightly more heat; drain excess liquid first
  • 1 cup (4 oz) shredded Monterey Jack cheese (113 g)
    • Pepper Jack cheese — 1:1 Adds extra heat and spice; melts equally well
  • 1 cup (4 oz) shredded cheddar cheese (113 g)
    • Colby Jack cheese — 1:1 Milder flavor with a similar melt; slightly less sharp
  • 3/4 cup sour cream (180 g)
    • Full-fat Greek yogurt — 1:1 Lower calorie and higher protein; slightly tangier flavor that mellows during cooking
  • 1 package (3 oz) cream cheese, softened (85 g)
    • Neufchâtel cheese — 1:1 Lower calorie with a lighter texture; nearly identical flavor and melting behavior
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder (8 g)
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin (1 g)
  • Tortilla chips, for serving
  • Salsa, for serving

Steps

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the refried beans, picante sauce, shredded Monterey Jack cheese, shredded cheddar cheese, sour cream, softened cream cheese, chili powder, and ground cumin. Stir until everything is thoroughly blended and no large lumps of cream cheese remain.
  2. Transfer the mixture to a slow cooker and spread it into an even layer.
  3. Cover the slow cooker and cook on high for 2 hours, or until the dip is fully heated through and the cheeses are completely melted. Stir once or twice during cooking to ensure even heating and a smooth consistency.
  4. Once hot and creamy, serve directly from the slow cooker set to warm. Accompany with tortilla chips and salsa for dipping.

Tips

  • Keep It Warm After the 2-hour cook time, switch the slow cooker to the 'warm' setting to keep the dip at ideal serving temperature throughout your party without overcooking it.
  • Soften the Cream Cheese Fully Make sure your cream cheese is fully softened to room temperature before mixing. This prevents lumps and ensures a silky smooth dip.
  • Customize the Heat Use mild, medium, or hot picante sauce to control the spice level. For extra kick, add a pinch of cayenne pepper or use Pepper Jack in place of Monterey Jack.
  • Make It Ahead You can mix all the ingredients the night before, refrigerate the mixture, and simply pour it into the slow cooker when ready. Add 15–20 extra minutes to the cook time if starting from cold.
