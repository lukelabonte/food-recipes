Quick Pickled Red Onions

Tangy, slightly sweet, and spiced with cinnamon and oregano. Put them on everything.

Overview Ingredients Steps Tips Nutrition Weight
  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Total 15 min
  • Servings 8
  • Method Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 cup rice vinegar (240 g)
  • 2 cups water (474 g)
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano (1 g)
  • 2 red onions, thinly sliced (300 g)
  • 2 teaspoons salt (12 g)
  • 1½ tablespoons sugar (19 g)

Steps

  1. Thinly slice the red onions and place them in a large heatproof jar or bowl.
  2. In a saucepan, combine the rice vinegar, water, sugar, salt, oregano, cinnamon sticks, and bay leaves.
  3. Heat the brine over medium heat until it reaches a simmer. Do not let it boil.
  4. Pour the hot brine over the sliced onions, making sure they're fully submerged.
  5. Let cool to room temperature, then cover and refrigerate for at least 1 day before eating.

Tips

  • Slice the onions as thinly and evenly as you can. A mandoline works great here.
  • These keep in the fridge for up to 2 weeks. The flavor gets better after the first couple of days.
  • Remove the cinnamon sticks and bay leaves after 2 to 3 days so the spice doesn't overpower the onions.
